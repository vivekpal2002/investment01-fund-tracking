## 💡 About This Project

**FundTrack** is a centralized personal finance, wealth management, and investment tracking web application engineered to give individuals complete clarity and control over their money. 

In today's fast-paced environment, personal finances are often fragmented across multiple bank accounts, cash wallets, mutual funds, equities, and recurring monthly expenses. FundTrack brings all of these fragmented pieces into a single, cohesive, and secure financial dashboard.

Whether you want to monitor everyday expenses against strict category budgets, track the performance of your stock and mutual fund investments, or automate milestones toward long-term life goals (like buying a house or building an emergency fund), FundTrack provides real-time insights, multi-currency support, and visual analytics to help you make smarter financial decisions.

---

### ❓ Why FundTrack?

- **Eliminates Financial Guesswork**: Replaces scattered spreadsheets and multiple banking apps with a unified source of truth.
- **Proactive Budgeting**: Set monthly spending caps by category and receive visual alerts before you overspend.
- **Holistic Portfolio View**: Track both liquid cash reserves and market-linked investments (stocks & mutual funds) side by side.
- **Goal-Driven Savings**: Visualize your progress toward financial milestones with completion percentages and target deadlines.
- **Tailored to You**: Switch effortlessly between multiple global currencies and dark/light display themes according to your preference.

### 🧩 Core Modules & What They Do

| Module | Purpose & Capabilities |

| 📑 **Reports & Analytics** | Interactive breakdowns comparing income vs. expenses, category distributions, and monthly cash flow. |
